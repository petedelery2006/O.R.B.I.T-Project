The notes also report **two protoboards for power rails** and **one protoboard for motor control**.

The firmware identifies the gas module as SGP30, climate sensor as AHT30, magnetic sensor as LSM303DLHC, pressure sensor as BMP280, and spectral sensor as AS7341. One Mega and two Uno sketches are supplied.

**Battery discrepancy:** the supplied list says “5V 5AH Deep Cycle Battery”; the Mega source assumes a 12 V 5 Ah LiFePO4 pack and uses 12.8 V nominal. Actual pack specification and installed quantity need confirmation.

Product links are retained as supplied references, not verified specifications or purchasing recommendations. RGB LED and buzzer entries link to this project rather than a specific product.

The [September original notes](sensors-original.md) remain as a historical snapshot. Their old unpurchased entries do not describe the current list. The O2 sensor appears only in those earlier notes and is absent from the current list and firmware.
